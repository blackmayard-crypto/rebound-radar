Understand the data.

This redesign preserves the original application’s 12 fixed stock records. Their prices, declines, causes, risk categories, and scores have no supplied market timestamp or documented scoring formula.

  • The opportunity score is a sample ranking, not a probability of profit.
  • TradingView supplies the interactive chart separately. Data may be delayed or unavailable for some symbols. The app does not use widget data to calculate scores or validate rebound signals.
  • Watchlists are stored in this browser only. They do not trigger alerts or sync across devices.
  • The external chart button opens Yahoo Finance for independent research.

Before using real signals, connect a market data provider and define, test, and document the scoring method.

Check daily price history

Import one ticker’s daily bars. The file stays in this browser and is cleared when you close this panel.

Use headers Date,Close,Volume and dates in YYYY-MM-DD format. Include at least 21 completed sessions, use a consistent split-adjusted close series, and import one row per trading date.

Pilot formula v1 gives up to 40 points for a decline of 20% from the prior 20-session closing high, 40 for a one-session gain of 5%, and 20 for volume at twice the prior 20-session average. Each component scales linearly from zero, is capped and rounded. Volume points start above average volume. The weights are experimental. These figures describe the imported history. They do not change the sample rankings or predict a rebound.
